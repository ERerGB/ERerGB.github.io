Drink ordering assistant

DrinkDrink

A Codex skill and offline tools for comparing verified drink checkout totals, offers, fulfillment options, and order progress.

View source and README on GitHub

Compare the real checkout, not the headline offer

A drink promotion is only useful when it applies to the chosen store, pickup or delivery route, and payment method. DrinkDrink compares complete observed quotes across available channels, including coupons, payment offers, fulfillment, and timing. Its offline comparator marks incomplete totals as provisional, so a cheaper-looking offer is not treated as a verified winner. Merchant login, payment, and order submission require a connected account or accessible app interface and the user’s authorization.

At a glance

Who it helps
People comparing coffee, tea, or other drink offers across available channels.
First useful action
Compare complete observed quotes and mark incomplete checkout totals as provisional.
Scope
The repository does not log in to merchants or submit an order on its own.
